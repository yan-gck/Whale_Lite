Good afternoon, Riverside Sports Centre, how can I help you? Hello, I would like to ask about joining the centre. Certainly. Are you interested in the swimming pool or the fitness classes? Mainly swimming, but I might try a class later. Could I take your name first? Yes, it is Marta Kowalski. Could you spell the surname for me? K O W A L S K I. Thank you. And what is your postcode? It is B H 4 2 L P. Right. Now, membership for an adult is thirty two pounds a month. That includes the pool and all the classes. Is there a cheaper option? There is an off peak membership at twenty pounds, but it can only be used before four o'clock on weekdays. I work in the mornings, so the off peak would suit me. Then the off peak it is. One thing to remember is that there is a joining fee of fifteen pounds, which you pay only once. Do I need to bring anything when I come? Yes, please bring a photo for your card, and a bank letter if you want to pay monthly. Also, the centre does not provide towels, so bring your own. Question one. What is the woman's surname? Question two. How much does off peak membership cost per month? Question three. When can off peak membership be used? Question four. How much is the joining fee? Question five. What must the woman bring for her card? Question six. What does the centre not provide?

Can I ask about the pool timetable? The pool is open from six in the morning until ten at night, but on Sundays it closes at eight. Is there a quiet time? The quietest time is between two and four in the afternoon on weekdays. In the evenings it is very busy. Do I need to book the pool? No, swimming is not booked, but the fitness classes must be booked at least twenty four hours in advance, and you can cancel up to two hours before without paying. Is there parking at the centre? Yes, there is free parking for members, but you need to display your card in the car. And finally, is there a cafe? There is a small cafe next to the reception, and it is open from eight until six. Perfect, thank you very much. Question seven. When does the pool close on Sundays? Question eight. When is the pool quietest? Question nine. How far in advance must fitness classes be booked? Question ten. Where should members display their card?
